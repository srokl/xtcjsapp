feat: add TOC chapter names from filenames in image-to-XTC pack

When packing multiple images into a single XTC/XTCH container, each
image's filename (without extension) is now used as the chapter title
in the table of contents. The TOC metadata with page ranges is passed
to both buildXtcFromBuffers and buildXtcHeaderAndIndex, so the packed
file has navigable chapters on the e-reader.
